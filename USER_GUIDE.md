# ImpactOS End-User Operational Handbook

Welcome to **ImpactOS** — the comprehensive operating system designed for Indian non-governmental organizations, CSR foundations, and social impact leaders.

---

## 📖 User Navigation Guide

### 1. Executive Dashboard (`Overview`)
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**: View live count of total beneficiaries reached, active projects, budget burn rates, grant funding raised, and volunteer hours.
- **Regional & Time Filters**: Filter telemetry by North Region (DL/HR/UP), West Region (MH/RJ), East Region (BR/WB), or Fiscal Year.

### 2. Beneficiary Management (`Beneficiaries`)
- **Registry & Search**: Search beneficiaries by name, code (`BEN-2026-DL-001`), phone number, or district.
- **Beneficiary Profile Drawer**: Click any beneficiary to inspect identity documents, program enrollments, session attendance logs, and audit timelines.

### 3. Data Importer (`Data Import`)
- **8-Stage Ingestion Importer**: Drag and drop `.csv` or `.xlsx` files to ingest beneficiary records.
- **Column Mapping & Pre-Ingestion Audit**: Interactively map CSV headers to database fields while flagging duplicate phone or Aadhaar records.

### 4. AI Impact Analyst & Query Assistant (`AI Insights`)
- **Telemetry Anomaly Alerts**: View automated database scans for duplicate beneficiaries, budget burn disparities, and attendance drops.
- **Ask ImpactOS**: Type natural language queries (*"Which program is underperforming?"*, *"Which district has the highest reach?"*) to receive answers with step-by-step mathematical calculations and evidence.

### 5. Automated Reporting (`Reports`)
- **Perspective Selection**: Select Executive Board Briefs, Donor 80G Tax Summaries, or CSR Section 135 Companies Act Compliance Reports.
- **Dual Export**: Click **Export PDF Brief** or **Download Excel (.xlsx)**.

### 6. Finance Module (`Finance`)
- **Budget vs Actual Variance**: Compare allocated program budgets against actual line-item expenses.
- **Cash Flow & Capital Runway**: Monitor capital inflow timeline and runway forecast.
